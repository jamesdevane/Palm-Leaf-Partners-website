How

Built honestly. Built carefully.

A 3-year-plus pre-production. No racing to ship. No shortcuts on the cultural work. No fake-it-til-you-make-it gameplay screenshots.

01

AI-Augmented, Human-Authored

Concept art, world-building, asset generation accelerated with AI tools — but every design decision goes through a cultural validation pass before it lands in canon. AI is a force-multiplier, not a substitute for judgment.

02

Real Congolese Voices in the Loop

UPTW members, Dr. Elie's network, real DRC consultants reviewing characters, dialogue, and cultural representation. "African voices in dev loop — not optional" is a design law, written into our canon repo.

03

Two Products. One World.

Mboka Mobile — F2P Android, Phaser engine, designed for African mainland reach.
Mboka Academy — Steam premium, Godot 4 + Terrain3D, an educational-grade simulation depth for diaspora and Western audiences. Shared canon. Different art floors. Same story.

04

3+ Year Timeline

Pre-production is locked. Vertical slice (Kinshasa) is the first build target. Tier-A provinces unlock one at a time. We will ship when the work is honest — not when the calendar says to.

Modernization ≠ Westernization

The DRC's future is built by Congolese hands, on Congolese soil, with Congolese governance. Mboka tells that story in the engine. The real work tells it on the ground.
